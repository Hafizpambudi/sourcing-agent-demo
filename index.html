The problem

A growth team has to find the right contact at hundreds of organisations, across dozens of markets, and repeat it every quarter. Finding them by hand means days of searching, cross-checking the CRM, and guessing whether a lead is worth a call.

The pipeline turns that into a repeatable flow. Agents search, read and score. People decide. Scraping is the easy part. The work is deciding where the machine stops.

What the machine owns

  • Searching and reading source pages
  • Extracting structured fields
  • Scoring against a fixed rubric
  • Deduplicating against the CRM
  • Drafting a first outreach email

What a human keeps

  • Qualifying a contact
  • Resolving fuzzy duplicates
  • Choosing the priority label
  • Opening the write gate
  • Setting the outreach sequence
